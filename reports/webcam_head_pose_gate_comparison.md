# Webcam pilot + ATC tower video: head-pose gate comparison

Exploratory engineering QC, Method A (fixed EAR thresholds). These are **not** validated blinks, PERCLOS80 or fatigue measurements, and no manual eyelid annotation exists yet. Full table, all three thresholds: [`webcam_head_pose_gate_comparison.csv`](webcam_head_pose_gate_comparison.csv), generated by `scripts/25_compare_head_pose_gate.py`.

## Setup (local execution, 2026-11-15)

- **Pilot:** 16 MJPEG 640×480 recordings (4 webcams × above/below × with/without glasses), 180 s each, one participant.
- **ATC tower:** one 1920×1080 H.264 recording, 567 s, 30 FPS, one controller at a working position (9 Nov 2026). The first frame has a −128 ms pre-roll PTS, which is accepted only on frame 1.
- **Extraction:** MediaPipe Face Landmarker VIDEO mode on every frame (script 23). The run stores eye landmarks and head pitch/yaw/roll.
- **Analysis:** script 24, with a 150 ms gap guard and a 500 ms minimum for sustained closure.
  - **Gate:** a frame is excluded as not assessable when the head pitch is more than 10° below the video's median pitch.
  - **Gate off:** the results are identical to the earlier run (all common columns are equal).

## Threshold 0.21

| Video | Excluded by gate | Blinks/min (no gate → gate) | Sustained candidates (no gate → gate) | PERCLOS-like (no gate → gate) |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| ATC tower | 15.9% | 19.2 → 21.8 | 76 → 18 | 0.248 → 0.059 |
| Pilot, 16 videos | 0.0–3.6% | small change | 0–15 → 0–15 | ≤0.128 → ≤0.128 |

## Observations

1. **ATC tower.**
   - The two longest pre-gate candidates on manual inspection were the controller looking down at the desk. The eyes were not closed.
   - The gate removes most such events, and the blink rate barely changes.
   - The longest remaining candidates are writing on paper with the eyes looking down while the head stays mostly level. The gate cannot detect gaze-only looks.
2. **Pilot.** The participant faced the camera, so the gate has little effect.
   - The 15 candidates in `atas_kacamata/cam2` remain. Their likely causes are glasses, light and small eye size, not head pose.
3. **Caution: the gate can also remove real closures.**
   - In `bawah_tanpa_kacamata`, one ~600 ms closure seen on all four cameras (galleries showed a genuine squeeze of the eyes) was excluded because the head dropped at the same time.
   - In drowsiness, head nodding often accompanies eye closure. Excluding head-down frames could therefore hide the most fatigue-relevant events.
   - Treat the gate as a review aid, not as a final filter. Review head-down closures separately rather than discarding them.
4. The 10° limit is uncalibrated. At ATC threshold 0.21 the sensitivity was:

   | Limit | Excluded time | Sustained candidates | PERCLOS-like |
   |---|---:|---:|---:|
   | 15° | 5% | 45 | 0.145 |
   | 10° | 16% | 18 | 0.059 |
   | 7° | 23% | 13 | 0.034 |
   | 5° | 30% | 10 | 0.022 |

## Limitations

- One participant in the pilot and one controller at ATC.
- Short recordings.
- No ground truth.
- The galleries and raw video stay local and are not published.
