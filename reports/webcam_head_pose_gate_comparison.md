# Webcam pilot + ATC tower videos: head-pose gate comparison

Exploratory engineering QC with Method A (fixed EAR thresholds). These are **not** validated blinks, PERCLOS80 or fatigue measurements, and no manual eyelid annotation exists yet. The full table covers all three thresholds: [`webcam_head_pose_gate_comparison.csv`](webcam_head_pose_gate_comparison.csv), generated by `scripts/25_compare_head_pose_gate.py`.

## Setup (local execution, 2026-11-15)

- **Pilot:** 16 MJPEG 640×480 recordings (4 webcams × above/below × with/without glasses), 180 s each, one participant.
- **ATC tower:** three 1920×1080 H.264 recordings at 30 FPS from 9 Nov 2026, one controller at a working position in each.

  | Recording | Duration | Controller | Script 23 options |
  |---|---:|---|---|
  | `11_13_04` | 567 s | A | Full frame |
  | `10_37_35` | 75 s | B | `--roi 480,0,960,810 --max-faces 3` |
  | `10_51_07` | 408 s | C | `--roi 640,100,960,800 --max-faces 3` |

  - **Why the crops:** in the full 1080p frame the face detector found the controller in 0/10 (`10_37_35`) and 1/20 (`10_51_07`) sampled frames. A fixed crop around the work position raised this to 8/10 and 13/20.
  - **Why `--max-faces 3`:** the operator is the largest detected face, which keeps background staff out of the measurement.
  - **Not processed:**
    - `10_32_56`: no operator facing the camera.
    - `10_39_20`: operator has their back to the camera.
    - `11_07_31`: 1.5 s accidental clip.
    - `11_10_44` and `10_59_16`: mostly profile views.
- **Extraction:** MediaPipe Face Landmarker VIDEO mode on every frame (script 23), saving eye landmarks and head pitch/yaw/roll.
- **Analysis:** script 24, with a 150 ms gap guard and a 500 ms minimum for sustained closures.
  - **Gate:** a frame is excluded as not assessable when the head pitch is more than 10° below the video's median.
  - **Gate off:** pilot results are identical to the earlier run.

### Duration tiers at threshold 0.21 (no gate → gate)

Counts of completed sustained-closure candidates per tier, plus the median share of blinks lasting 300–500 ms per video. The tier boundaries are exploratory reference points:
- **1 s** is the lower bound of Euro NCAP SD-202 v1.2 microsleep (the manufacturer declares 1–2 s).
- **3 s** is the Euro NCAP sleep threshold.
- **300–500 ms** long blinks follow Caffier et al. 2003.

| Set | 0.5–1 s | 1–3 s | ≥ 3 s | Long-blink share (median) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ATC `11_13_04` | 29 → 13 | 37 → 2 | 10 → 3 | 0.18 → 0.16 |
| ATC `10_51_07` | 3 → 2 | 9 → 5 | 5 → 0 | 0.09 → 0.10 |
| ATC `10_37_35` | 4 → 3 | 5 → 4 | 1 → 1 | 0.19 → 0.19 |
| Pilot (16 videos) | 37 → 31 | 15 → 12 | 2 → 2 | 0.08 → 0.07 |

Without the gate, controllers who were working had up to 10 "≥ 3 s closures" per video, which would meet the Euro NCAP sleep definition. These were looking-down episodes. A longer duration threshold does not remove such artefacts; pose/gaze handling is what reduces them.

## Observations

1. **Looking down is the dominant false positive in real ATC work.** It showed up in all three controllers. In the longest pre-gate galleries the controller looks down at the desk or strips (writing) and the eyes are not closed.
   - When the head tilts clearly, e.g. `10_51_07` E0051 at +15° for 9.2 s, the gate removes the event.
   - When mainly the eyes look down (head only +5–9°, as in `10_37_35` E0012 and `11_13_04` E0271), the event survives. The gate cannot see gaze-only looks.
2. **Face availability is much lower at ATC than in the pilot** (60–90% vs ~100%). The controllers turn to the windows, other staff and the strip board.
   - **Small faces in the wide frame:** a full 1080p view can make the detector miss a face that is clearly visible, so ROI cropping was necessary.
   - **No relabelling of missing faces:** missing-face periods are left as gaps, never as closures.
3. **Pilot.** The participant faced the camera, so the gate has little effect.
   - The 15 candidates in `atas_kacamata/cam2` remain. Their likely causes are glasses, light and small eye size.
4. **Caution: the gate can also remove real closures.** In `bawah_tanpa_kacamata`, one ~600 ms closure seen on all four cameras was excluded because the head dropped at the same time.
   - In drowsiness, head nodding often accompanies eye closure. Treat the gate as a review aid, not as a final filter.
5. **The 10° limit is uncalibrated.** At `11_13_04`, threshold 0.21, the sensitivity was:

   | Limit | Excluded time | Sustained candidates | PERCLOS-like |
   |---|---:|---:|---:|
   | 15° | 5% | 45 | 0.145 |
   | 10° | 16% | 18 | 0.059 |
   | 7° | 23% | 13 | 0.034 |
   | 5° | 30% | 10 | 0.022 |

## Limitations

- One pilot participant and three ATC controllers, each in one short recording.
- The ROIs were chosen by inspecting sampled frames.
- No ground truth.
- Galleries and raw video stay local and are not published.
