# Exploratory Method B: per-person closure % and PERCLOS80 with ESTIMATED references

Local execution, 2026-11-16, generated by `scripts/27_method_b_explore.py`. The full table is in [`webcam_method_b_explore.csv`](webcam_method_b_explore.csv): 19 videos × closed-reference percentiles 5/10/25. These results are **exploratory**. None of the recordings has an instructed eye-closure segment, so the closed-eye reference is estimated. This is **not calibrated PERCLOS80** and not a fatigue score.

## Method

- **Open-eye EAR:** median over valid frames in the first 5 min, keeping only frames with the head within 5° of that window's median pitch.
- **Blink troughs:** runs where EAR drops below 60% of the open EAR, lasting ≤ 500 ms, complete on both sides, with no gap > 150 ms and a neutral head throughout. This relative rule does not depend on the fixed 0.21 threshold.
- **Closed-eye EAR (estimate):** the 10th percentile of the trough values (5th and 25th as sensitivity). A video needs ≥ 10 troughs, otherwise Method B is not applied.
- **Closure % and PERCLOS80:** closure % = (open − EAR) / (open − closed) × 100. PERCLOS80 is the time-weighted share of assessable time with closure ≥ 80%, counting all closures including blinks and respecting the 150 ms gap guard. Blink and sustained-closure candidates use the per-person EAR at 80% closure.

## Observations

1. **Method B removes threshold-noise artefacts.**
   - `atas_kacamata/cam2` has an open EAR of only 0.226, so its 15 Method A sustained candidates at 0.21 drop to 0 once the threshold is scaled to that person and camera.
2. **Method B under-counts blinks at low resolution or frame rate.**
   - In the pilot (640×480, 21–30 FPS), blink rates fall to 0.3–10.6/min. Many natural blinks never reach the estimated 80% closure in the sampled frames.
   - At ATC 1080p/30 FPS the blink rate is unchanged (~21/min).
   - A lower closure level (e.g. 50%) might suit blink counting better, with 80% kept for PERCLOS. This is untested.
3. **Method B does not solve looking down.** ATC PERCLOS80 stays high without the head gate (0.14–0.20). The gate lowers it, and gaze-only downward looks still remain.
4. **Coverage.**
   - 7 of 19 videos do not have enough neutral-head blinks to estimate a closed reference.
   - The estimate is sensitive to the chosen percentile: ATC 10:51 PERCLOS80 without the gate is 0.191 / 0.204 / 0.252 for the 5th / 10th / 25th percentile.
5. **Conclusion.** Estimated references are useful for exploration and for removing fixed-threshold artefacts. They are not a substitute for an instructed eye-closure segment, which gives a known closed EAR and a validation set.

## Limitations

- One pilot participant and three ATC controllers.
- The closed-eye EAR is estimated from data that also contains partial blinks.
- The open-eye window assumes the first 5 min are alert.
- No manual annotation exists. All values are candidates, not validated measurements.
